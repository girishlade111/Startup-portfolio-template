Hi, I'm Girish 👋

Born in Pandharpur, Maharashtra, and now building from Mumbai — I'm a solo founder, full-stack developer, and automation test engineer by profession. By night (and weekends), I build LadeStack: a growing suite of free developer tools powered by AI. No investors. No co-founders. Just code, conviction, and coffee.

I work in the IT sector specializing in automation and manual testing. Outside of my 9-to-5, I've been quietly building products that thousands of developers use for free — because great tools shouldn't cost a fortune.

LADESTACK

LadeStack

Every great tool was once just an idea someone couldn't afford to build.

Growing up in Pandharpur and moving to Mumbai with nothing but ambition, I saw how expensive, paywalled, and login-heavy developer tools created barriers for people like me — developers with big ideas but limited resources.

So I built LadeStack. Not with investor money. Not with a team. Just me, my laptop, and a stubborn belief that powerful tools should be free and accessible to every developer on earth — no credit card, no login, no catch.

Every product I ship is a promise: whatever I build, you'll always be able to use it for free. Because the best way to grow as a developer is to have access to great tools — and I'm here to make sure you do.

From Pandharpur to the Internet. For every developer who ever said "I wish I had this for free."

MY JOURNEY

Building in Public

From Pandharpur to the Internet — one commit at a time.

2023

The Spark 🔥

Realized developers needed free, no-login tools. Started learning and building in spare time while working full-time in IT.

Early 2024

LadeStack is Born 🚀

Officially launched LadeStack as a brand. Registered the domain, built the foundation, started shipping the first tools.

Mid 2024

First Tools Go Live ✅

Shipped the Code Editor, Land Converter, and Resume Builder — three fully free, no-login tools used by real developers.

Late 2024

Going AI 🤖

Started building AI-powered products: LadeStack Builder (AI website generator) and an AI-powered image editor — both in active development.

2025

Building the Stack 🏗️

Expanding LadeStack into a full developer toolkit. Self-hosted infrastructure on DigitalOcean, Supabase backend, n8n automations. 500+ tools in the arsenal.

Now ∞

Still Building 🔥

Every week, a new feature. Every month, a new product. Solo, bootstrapped, and unstoppable. LadeStack is just getting started.
